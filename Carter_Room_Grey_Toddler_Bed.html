Letters can be dragged even while locked. Hold Shift and drag to rotate, or use Q / E when selected. Drop near a slot to snap back in.

Pick a .glb, or a .gltf together with its .bin and textures. You can also drop files on the page.

Character

Character remains selectable while the room is locked. Arrow keys or touch joystick walk; Space jumps. The blue ring marks selection.
